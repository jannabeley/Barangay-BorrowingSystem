Terms and Conditions

Barangay Camp Tinio Equipment Borrowing System

Please read the following before using the system:

  1. Borrowers must be residents of Barangay Camp Tinio and must attach a clear photo of a valid ID with every request.
  2. All information you provide (name, contact number, purpose, dates) must be true and accurate.
  3. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval. Requests are reviewed and may be approved or rejected by barangay staff.
  4. Equipment that is currently out on loan is not available to other borrowers until it has been returned.
  5. Borrowed items may only be used for the purpose stated in the request and must be returned on or before the return date, in the same condition.
  6. Lost or damaged items must be reported to the barangay immediately. The borrower may be required to repair or replace them.
  7. Your personal information and ID photo are used only to process and monitor equipment borrowing.
  8. The barangay may reserve equipment for official barangay activities and may cancel requests when necessary.
